H2Factory

H2Factory runs the matrix-vector product for a hierarchical fast method. It walks the trees, applies near and far interactions, and manages moments and scratch. Your method supplies the numerical data and operations.

Two ways into the guide

Traits and dispatch explains how the small types and policies select operations throughout the components below.

To understand the code, start with How the multiplication works. Then follow the components below.

To implement a method, start with Required methods. The worked example connects those methods into a small complete map. It needs no application-scale assembly.

Construction and execution connects method construction, workspace allocation, and multiplication.

ComponentWhat it covers
MapsShared core data, workspaces, accessors, and scheduling
MomentsLeaf collections, moment types, allocation, and storage reuse
Near interactionsSelecting, computing, and storing direct interactions
Far interactionsLevel transfers, I2O coupling, modulation, and traversal
DiagnosticsStructural checks, map summaries, and timings

The API reference provides individual signatures. Most extension hooks are qualified, for example H2Factory.buildmoments. Tree functions come from H2Trees, imported separately.

H2Factory does not choose a physical kernel, interpolation basis, or quadrature rule for every method. Those choices belong to the implementation. Optional extensions provide BEAST assembly and FFT-based coupling.
